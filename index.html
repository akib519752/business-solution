FAQ

Questions we hear often.

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp.

Do I need technical knowledge to use your software?

No. We design for everyday business owners and staff, and we train your team during setup.

Can the stock app work on my mobile phone?

Yes. Our apps work on Android phones, tablets and computers, so you can check stock and sales from anywhere.

Can you customise the software for my business?

Yes. We can add fields, reports, branches or completely new modules to match how you work.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. A simple website or ready stock app setup is quick; custom software takes longer. You'll get a timeline with your quote.

What happens after launch?

We stay with you. Support, fixes and updates are handled over WhatsApp or phone, and we can add new features as you grow.

How do I pay?

We accept bank transfer, bKash and Nagad. Payment is usually split into stages across the project.
